DLSS5-NR-Nexus
==============

Project:  DLSS5-NR-Nexus by williamstosh (Carlos Lopez Jr.)
Source:   https://github.com/williamstosh/DLSS5-NR-Nexus (commit 8fabd388e9)

What is derived from it
-----------------------

The exposure governor (retoque/dlssnr/DlssNr_ExposureGovernor.h, used by
retoque/shaders/dlssnr/DlssNr_Dx12.cpp) takes its rule from the normalization governor in
shaders/v6_autoscale.cs_5_1.hlsl and dlssnr.hpp: the applied divisor is rate-limited toward a fresh
estimate with asymmetric per-second limits (4 stops/s up, 0.5 stops/s down), a hysteresis hold (engage at
0.15 stop, track until within 0.02), a hold on a frame that cannot be read, a re-prime on the game's reset,
and one state per stream.

Detail stability (retoque/dlssnr/DlssNr_DetailStability.h, retoque/shaders/dlssnr/DlssNr_DetailStability_Dx12.*,
retoque/shaders/dlssnr/precompile/dlssnr_detail_stability*) takes its filter from shaders/look_compose.cs_5_1.hlsl and
dlssnr.hpp: a history of the log edit, reprojected with the game's motion vectors, clipped to the current 3x3 range of
the edit and rejected where the reprojected input leaves the current 3x3 range of the input, blended at alpha 0.15.

What is not theirs: the governor's host-side state and its clock, its unreadable-frame test (the game's own implied
white point, not a frame histogram), the pause rule, and where each sits in the renderer; the detail filter's
per channel edit on the encoded proxy and answer, its near binary band in c / (1 + c), its half-rounded one-channel
history and its exact repeat. See docs/dlssnr/design/exposure-governor.md and docs/dlssnr/design/detail-stability.md.
