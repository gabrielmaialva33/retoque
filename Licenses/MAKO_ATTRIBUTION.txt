MAKO: adaptive frame-generation pacing rules
=============================================

retoque/lsfg/presenter/MfgAdaptive.h reimplements, for the LSFG MFG presenter's
log-only adaptive multiplier shadow, algorithms of MAKO:

  https://github.com/eugeniosegala/MAKO   (read at commit a321c2a)

What is derived from it
-----------------------

  * RefreshBudget -- FixedRefreshBudget::plan
    (engine/mako-render/src/presentation_policy.hpp): the interval average,
    the per-real-frame refresh credit, the one-output carry and the 250 ms
    gap reset.

  * CadenceEstimator -- the cadence observation of AdaptiveScheduler
    (engine/mako-render/src/adaptive_scheduler.cpp): burst exclusion at
    max(3x baseline, 2x target), the 10 fps stall floor, and the three-frame
    confirmation of a 2x cadence drop held in a shadow estimate.

  * DeliveryWindow -- GeneratedDeliveryWindow
    (engine/mako-render/src/generated_frame_delivery.hpp): the integer 5%
    miss tolerance.

  * HigherLoadIsUseful / OutcomeAt -- adaptiveHigherLoadIsUseful and
    adaptiveLoadOutcome (engine/mako-render/src/adaptive_scheduler.cpp): a
    higher load must add more output than the real frames it costs, plus 2%
    of the target from x3.

No MAKO source is copied; the code is rewritten against Retoque's presenter,
with MAKO's constants. The test vectors in tests/lsfg-adaptive restate MAKO's
own expectations (engine/mako-render/tests/presentation_policy_tests.cpp and
adaptive_scheduler_tests.cpp). Retoque is distributed under the GNU General
Public License version 3 (see LICENSE), with which GPL-3.0-or-later code may be
combined.
